02.5 / About Foodvio

公司简介 About Foodvio

北京花花食界食品科技有限公司成立于 2021 年 8 月,总部位于北京,是国内领先的专业西餐食品供应链品牌与西式餐饮一体化解决方案服务商。公司以"还原世界味道,享受美食乐趣"及"三好一高"(食材好、口味好、服务好、还原度高)为产品理念,为餐饮企业、大型 KA 客户及家庭消费者提供标准化西餐产品方案与一站式定制服务。 Beijing Foodvio Food Technology Co., Ltd. was founded in August 2021 and is headquartered in Beijing — a leading professional Western cuisine supply-chain brand and integrated Western foodservice solutions provider in China. Guided by "restoring world flavors, sharing the joy of good food" and our "Three Goods, One High" philosophy (great ingredients, great taste, great service, high authenticity), we deliver standardized Western cuisine programs and one-stop custom services for foodservice operators, major KA clients and families.

  • · 成立于 2021 年 8 月,总部北京,专业西餐食品供应链品牌Founded August 2021, headquartered in Beijing — a professional Western cuisine supply-chain brand
  • · 4 大生产基地近 10 万㎡:江西赣州 6.6 万㎡ · 河北张家口烘焙产业园 1.6 万㎡ · 河北邢台西式酱料工厂 8,000 ㎡ · 北京研发中心 6,000 ㎡Four production bases totaling nearly 100,000㎡: Ganzhou 66,000㎡ · Zhangjiakou Bakery Park 16,000㎡ · Xingtai Western Sauce Plant 8,000㎡ · Beijing R&D Center 6,000㎡
  • · 100+ SKU,四大产品系列:西式酱料、西餐主食、炸品小食、主菜大餐100+ SKUs across four product families: Western sauces, Western staples, fried snacks and main courses
  • · 3,000+ 合作伙伴,覆盖全国 10 余省市3,000+ partners across 10+ provinces and cities in China
  • · 资质:HACCP、ISO 22000、BSCI、美国 FDA 注册,4 大基地均具备对应 SC 生产许可Credentials: HACCP, ISO 22000, BSCI and US FDA registration; all four bases hold corresponding SC production licenses
