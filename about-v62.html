01 / Who We Are

我们是谁 Who We Are

北京花花食界食品科技有限公司(Foodvio)成立于 2021 年 8 月,总部位于北京,是国内领先的西式餐饮一体化解决方案服务商及家庭餐饮品牌提供商。创始人兼 CEO 陈建华拥有 22 年联想系工作经验及 15 年以上全球化管理经验,曾任 A 股大型食品上市公司总裁。 Beijing Foodvio Food Technology Co., Ltd. was founded in August 2021 and is headquartered in Beijing — a leading provider of integrated Western foodservice solutions and family dining brands in China. Founder & CEO Chen Jianhua brings 22 years within the Lenovo ecosystem, 15+ years of global management experience, and formerly served as president of a major A-share listed food company.

公司秉持"三好一高"(食材好、口味好、服务好、还原度高),由米其林主厨团队提供技术指导,融合真空冷冻干燥、超高压灭菌、速冻锁鲜、益生菌发酵、气调保鲜等食品科技,风味还原度达 95%。整合新疆面粉、黑龙江五常大米、内蒙古牛羊肉、江西螺丝椒与黄鸡、云南松露迷迭香等国内优质产区资源,并引进挪威三文鱼、智利鲑鱼、俄罗斯与美国帝王蟹等全球食材。 Guided by our "Three Goods, One High" philosophy (great ingredients, great taste, great service, high authenticity) and Michelin-chef technical direction, we combine food technologies — vacuum freeze-drying, ultra-high-pressure sterilization, quick-freeze freshness-lock, probiotic fermentation, modified-atmosphere preservation — to reach 95% flavor authenticity. We integrate premium Chinese origins (Xinjiang flour, Wuchang rice, Inner Mongolian beef & lamb, Jiangxi chili & chicken, Yunnan truffle & rosemary) alongside global ingredients such as Norwegian salmon, Chilean salmon and king crab from Russia and the US.

依托江西、河北张家口、河北邢台、北京 4 大生产基地近 10 万平方米产能,目前已上市 100 余款西餐产品,累计服务客户 3,000+,覆盖全国十余省市,并与中国石油、中国石化、国航、首都机场餐饮集团、北京高校联合采购平台等达成战略合作。旗下 B2C 品牌"华仔爸爸(ChinnPaPa)"聚焦儿童健康营养食品。 Backed by four production bases (Jiangxi, Zhangjiakou & Xingtai in Hebei, and Beijing) totaling nearly 100,000㎡, we have launched 100+ Western cuisine products, served 3,000+ clients across 10+ provinces, and built strategic partnerships with PetroChina, Sinopec, Air China, the Capital Airport catering group and Beijing's university joint procurement platform. Our B2C brand ChinnPaPa focuses on healthy, nutritious food for children.
